What the app does

  • Scan paper into searchable PDFs, with on-device text recognition in English and Vietnamese.
  • Read, annotate, fill forms and sign PDFs. Merge, split, reorder, compress and convert them.
  • Create and edit documents, spreadsheets and presentations that export to DOCX, XLSX and PPTX.
  • Document AI on supported devices: cited summaries, data extraction and rewriting. It runs on the device first and may use Apple Private Cloud Compute; “On-device only” in Settings keeps everything on the device.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account? No. The app has no account and no developer server.

What does it cost? Every feature is unlocked for the first 60 days. After that, each metered feature can be used 3 times a month for free. Opening, reading, annotating and signing stay free. Premium (monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase) removes the monthly limits. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; manage them in your Apple Account settings.

How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings > Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription? On iPhone or iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Mac: App Store > your name > Account Settings > Subscriptions.

Where are my files? On your device, or in your own iCloud Drive if you choose it. We never receive them.
