ElizaAI Office Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · Applies to the Android app and the iOS / macOS apps · Tiếng Việt bên dưới

This page covers every platform. Jump to Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac or Tiếng Việt. The apps differ in how AI and purchases work, so each platform has its own section; a section applies only to its own platform.

Android app

Summary

ElizaAI Office has no account and no developer server. We (the developer) do not receive your documents, scans, signatures, profile or AI requests. Your files stay on your device. The only data that leaves the app is described below: optional AI requests that you start with your own key, diagnostics sent by Google’s ML Kit components, and what Google Play itself handles for purchases.

Documents and scans

Documents are stored in the app’s private storage on your device, or opened in place from the location you choose through Android’s Storage Access Framework (for example Google Drive, OneDrive or Files). There is no sync service run by us. Photos are imported through the system Photo Picker, so the app does not ask for storage or photo-library permission. Scanning, text recognition (OCR), conversions, formula calculation and document editing run on your device.

Android Auto Backup is enabled for the app, so Google may back up the app’s private files (including your library) to your own Google account according to your device’s backup settings. That backup belongs to you and Google; we cannot access it.

AI features

Each AI request is handled by the first of these that applies:

  • Deterministic — tables, formulas, translation rules and similar work are done by app code on the device. No model, no network.
  • On device — on supported phones the app uses Gemini Nano through Google’s ML Kit GenAI APIs (Android AICore). The text is processed on the device. Android may first download the feature’s model to the device.
  • Your own model — off by default. If you turn it on, accept the consent screen and enter your own API key, then for heavy tasks the app can send the text of the document you chose to Anthropic’s Messages API (api.anthropic.com), using your key and your account with that provider. Before each document, the app shows a confirmation with the model, the host and how many characters will be sent, and nothing is sent unless you say yes. Your key is stored encrypted with the Android Keystore. Turning on “On-device only” in Settings blocks this tier completely. We never see the key, the text or the answer, and we do not run a proxy. The provider’s own privacy policy governs what it does with the request.
  • None — if no tier is available, the feature explains why and nothing is sent.

Every AI result is shown to you as a proposal that you accept or reject.

Google components that process or send data

  • ML Kit (text recognition, document scanner, GenAI). Image and document content is processed on the device; the document scanner runs inside Google Play services on the device. According to Google’s ML Kit data disclosure, the ML Kit SDK also sends device and app information (manufacturer, model, OS version, package name and version), performance metrics (such as latency), API configuration and per-installation identifiers to Google over HTTPS for diagnostics. These are not intended to identify you and do not include your document content. See developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.
  • Google Play Billing handles purchases (see below).

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. We receive no card or payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases your Google account owns each time it starts, and keeps a short-lived copy of the result on the device so it works offline. Cancel or manage subscriptions in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions).

The first 60 days are unlocked inside the app; after that, some features have a monthly free-use allowance. The start date and usage counters are kept only on your device, in the encrypted store, and are excluded from backup. Because of that, uninstalling and reinstalling the app starts a new free period on that device.

Signatures, profile and keys

Saved signatures, your autofill profile, your optional AI key, the purchase cache and the free-period data are stored encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. This store is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not move to a new phone. Ordinary settings (for example whether you gave AI consent) can be restored by backup, but the key is not, so the optional AI tier stays unavailable until you enter a key again.

Analytics and ads

ElizaAI Office has no analytics SDK, no advertising and no tracking. Crash and performance data, if you have allowed it in Android, comes from Google Play’s Android vitals, which Google collects under its own policy.

Permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET) and network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — to send an AI request to your own model after your confirmation, and to know whether the device is online.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — to buy and restore Premium through Google Play.
  • AICore (com.google.android.apps.aicore.service.BIND_SERVICE) — to use Gemini Nano on the device.
  • Run at startup, wake lock, foreground service — added by Android libraries for background work and home-screen widgets (refreshing the Recent Documents widget, finishing saves). They do not collect data.
  • Business cards are added to Contacts only through the system “New Contact” screen, which you confirm. The app has no contacts permission.

Children

ElizaAI Office is a general productivity app and is not directed to children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its private files and the encrypted store from the device. Files you saved elsewhere through the Storage Access Framework stay where you saved them. To remove a Google backup, use Google’s backup settings. To remove anything sent to your AI provider, contact that provider.

iPhone, iPad and Mac app

Effective November 20, 2026. This section applies to the App Store apps only.

Summary

ElizaAI Office has no account and no developer server. Your documents, scans, signatures and AI requests are not collected by us. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Documents and scans

Files are stored on your device or, if you turn on iCloud Drive, in your own iCloud account. Text recognition (OCR), conversions, translation and document editing run on your device.

Recordings, dictation and transcripts

When you dictate or record a meeting, the microphone audio is turned into text on your device by Apple’s speech recognition. Audio is never sent to us or to any server. Meeting recordings and their transcripts are saved as documents in your library, on your device or in your own iCloud, and are deleted when you delete the document.

Collaboration

If you choose Share > Collaborate, the document is shared through your iCloud account with the people you invite, under Apple’s iCloud terms. Comments and tracked changes are stored inside the document. Documents people share with you in Messages can appear in Shared with You. We receive none of this.

Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, AI features use Apple’s on-device model first. For longer documents they may use Apple Private Cloud Compute, which Apple operates without storing your data. You can turn on “On-device only” in Settings so AI never uses the network. ElizaAI Office does not send prompts or documents to any other provider.

Signatures and autofill profile

Saved signatures and your autofill profile are stored in the device Keychain, on this device only.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details.

Analytics

ElizaAI Office contains no analytics or tracking code. Apple may provide the developer aggregated App Store statistics under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Camera — to scan paper documents into PDFs.
  • Microphone — for dictation, meeting recording and slide audio, only after you tap to start.
  • Speech Recognition — to turn dictation and recordings into text on your device.
  • Photos you pick in the system photo picker are imported as pages; the app has no other access to your photo library and asks for no Photos permission.
  • Business cards are added to Contacts only through the system’s “New Contact” screen, which you confirm.

Children

ElizaAI Office is a general productivity app and is not directed to children.

Retention, deleting your data and withdrawing consent

We do not keep any of your data, because we never receive it. Everything the app stores stays on your device or in your own iCloud account.

  • Delete the app to remove its library, settings, and the Keychain items it created (saved signatures, autofill profile, and the free-period and usage counters). Reinstalling on the same device may restore the free-period record from the Keychain.
  • Files in iCloud Drive or Files stay where you saved them until you delete them in the Files app or in iCloud settings.
  • AI requests that use Apple Private Cloud Compute are processed by Apple and are not stored by Apple; see Apple’s Private Cloud Compute documentation. Turn on “On-device only” in the app’s Settings to stop AI from using the network.
  • Recordings and transcripts are deleted with the document that holds them.
  • Camera, Microphone and Speech Recognition access can be withdrawn at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security (iPhone and iPad) or System Settings > Privacy & Security (Mac).
  • Questions or requests: email or call the contact below.

Tính năng AI dùng mô hình trên thiết bị của Apple trước, sau đó có thể dùng Private Cloud Compute của Apple; bạn có thể bật “Chỉ trên thiết bị” trong Cài đặt. Chữ ký và hồ sơ tự điền được lưu trong Keychain trên thiết bị này. Thanh toán do Apple xử lý.

Micrô và nhận dạng giọng nói: khi bạn đọc chính tả hoặc ghi âm một cuộc họp, âm thanh được chuyển thành văn bản ngay trên thiết bị của bạn bằng tính năng Nhận dạng giọng nói của Apple và không bao giờ được gửi cho chúng tôi hay bất kỳ máy chủ nào. Bản ghi âm cuộc họp và bản chép lời của chúng được lưu dưới dạng tài liệu trong thư viện của bạn, trên thiết bị của bạn hoặc trong iCloud của chính bạn, và sẽ bị xóa khi bạn xóa tài liệu đó.

Cộng tác: nếu bạn chọn Chia sẻ > Cộng tác, tài liệu sẽ được chia sẻ qua tài khoản iCloud của bạn với những người bạn mời, theo điều khoản iCloud của Apple. Các bình luận và thay đổi được theo dõi được lưu trữ bên trong tài liệu. Chúng tôi không nhận được bất kỳ dữ liệu nào trong số này.

Ảnh bạn chọn trong trình chọn ảnh của hệ thống sẽ được nhập vào dưới dạng các trang; ứng dụng không có quyền truy cập nào khác vào thư viện Ảnh của bạn.

Lưu giữ và xóa dữ liệu: chúng tôi không lưu giữ dữ liệu nào của bạn vì không bao giờ nhận được chúng. Xóa ứng dụng sẽ xóa thư viện, cài đặt và các mục Keychain do ứng dụng tạo (chữ ký, hồ sơ tự điền, bộ đếm kỳ miễn phí). Tệp trong iCloud Drive hoặc Tệp vẫn ở nơi bạn lưu cho đến khi bạn xóa. Yêu cầu AI qua Private Cloud Compute do Apple xử lý và không được Apple lưu trữ; bật “Chỉ trên thiết bị” để AI không dùng mạng. Bạn có thể thu hồi quyền truy cập Máy ảnh, Micrô và Nhận dạng giọng nói bất cứ lúc nào trong Cài đặt > Quyền riêng tư & Bảo mật.
